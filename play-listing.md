## Full description

**TinyScan turns your phone into a document scanner — 100% offline. No account, no cloud upload, no watermark on your PDFs.**

Other scanner apps force you to create an account, upload sensitive documents to their servers, or stamp a watermark on every free page. TinyScan does one job, on your device, and gets out of the way.

**Why people switch to TinyScan:**
- ✅ **True offline** — works in airplane mode, your documents never leave your phone
- 📄 **Auto edge detection** — finds the document, drag corners to fine-tune
- 🎨 **Enhance modes** — color, grayscale, or crisp B&W
- 📚 **Multi-page PDFs** — scan 2, 5, 20 pages into one clean PDF
- 🚫 **No watermark** — ever. Your scan stays yours
- 🔒 **Privacy first** — no account, no cloud, only the photos you choose
- 📱 **Lightweight** — no bloat

**Perfect for:**
- Receipts, invoices, and expense reports
- Contracts and forms that need a clean PDF
- School forms, ID cards, whiteboards, notes

**How it works:**
1. Point the camera at a document (or import a photo)
2. Edges auto-detect — drag corners to adjust
3. Pick color, grayscale, or B&W
4. Add pages, tap Save — PDF lands in Downloads/TinyScan

No account. No cloud. No watermark. Just scanning.
